# Project Overview — 256 Logistics Compliance Hub

## Business Context

256 Logistics is a UK-based HGV transport operator required to comply with:

- **Home Office** — Sponsor Licence obligations for sponsored workers
- **DVSA** — Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency roadworthiness requirements
- **Traffic Commissioner** — Operator licence conditions and public inquiry evidence
- **Employment Law** — Contracts, Right to Work, disciplinary records

Currently, operators manage these obligations across multiple disconnected tools (spreadsheets, generic HR software like BrightHR, paper records). This creates compliance gaps, missed deadlines, and inability to produce coherent audit evidence on demand.

---

## Problem Statement

UK HGV operators face serious legal and financial risk when they cannot prove:

- That every employee has a valid Right to Work check on file
- That sponsored workers' conditions (salary, hours, location) match their visa terms
- That every vehicle has a current PMI, MOT, and insurance
- That defects are reported, reviewed, and closed within a compliant timeline
- That drivers are trained, briefed, and have signed required policy documents
- That management decisions (VOR authorisations, defect closures) are signed off and auditable

**Consequences of failure** include licence revocation, DVSA prohibition notices, Traffic Commissioner public inquiries, Home Office civil penalties, and insurance voidance.

---

## Solution

**256 Logistics Compliance Hub** is a purpose-built web application for UK HGV transport compliance.

It replaces BrightHR and generic fleet spreadsheets with one controlled platform that:

- Maintains complete employee and sponsored worker records
- Tracks all document expiry dates and fires alerts before deadlines
- Manages vehicle roadworthiness, PMI scheduling, and defect cases
- Records driver training, policy acknowledgements, and toolbox talks
- Scores compliance health across people, fleet, and driver categories
- Generates one-click audit packs for Home Office and DVSA inspections
- Creates a full immutable audit trail of every management action

## The 5 Modules

### Module 1: People & Compliance
Replaces BrightHR. Transport and Home Office-focused HR. Manages employee profiles, document uploads, Right to Work checks, sponsored worker conditions, Home Office alerts, and the Home Office Audit Pack.

### Module 2: Safety & Competency
Proves drivers are trained and aware of company procedures. Manages training matrices, digital policy acknowledgements, toolbox talk records, CPC tracking, and training alerts.

### Module 3: Fleet & Roadworthiness
DVSA-focused vehicle management. Manages vehicle profiles, maintenance records, PMI planner, daily walk-around checks, defect cases, VOR (Vehicle Off Road) records, and DVSA Audit Pack.

### Module 4: Driver Risk Management
Identifies weak drivers before they become a compliance risk. Manages driver risk scores, debrief records, and driver-specific compliance alerts (licence checks, CPC expiry, tacho card expiry, infringement history).

### Module 5: Audit & Legal Evidence Centre
The system's strongest differentiator. Maintains ready-made evidence folders for Home Office, DVSA, Traffic Commissioner, insurance claims, accident investigations, and employment disputes. Full immutable audit trail.

## Compliance Scoring System

The app gives 256 Logistics a live compliance score across three categories:

| Category | Scoring Factors |
|----------|----------------|
| People Compliance | Complete employee records, Right to Work checks, contracts, absence monitoring, sponsored worker compliance |
| Fleet Compliance | PMI compliance, MOT compliance, defect closure rate, VOR control, brake test evidence |
| Driver Compliance | Training completion, tachograph behaviour, walk-around check rate, licence checks, debriefs |

An **Overall Compliance Score** combines all three into a single health indicator for the business.
